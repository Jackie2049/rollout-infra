## 09:00 — Transformer Engine FP8源码深度阅读

### 核心

TE FP8训练 = Recipe体系 + Quantizer执行 + StateManager协调 + FP8 GEMM

关键发现:
- **Recipe体系**: DelayedScaling(Ada/Hopper, amax_history+scale=(FP8_MAX/amax)/2^margin) → Float8CurrentScaling(即时amax) → MXFP8BlockScaling(Blackwell) → NVFP4BlockScaling(FP4!)
- **Float8Quantizer**: per-tensor delayed scaling → tex.quantize(CUDA kernel) → scale×cast→FP8
- **FP8GlobalStateManager**: 全局状态+global_amax_buffer → autocast_exit集中reduce → fused kernel!
- **TE.Linear**: LinearFwdArgs打包 → input_quantizer→weight_quantizer→general_gemm(FP8 cublasLt) → output
- **Userbuffers**: comm+GEMM overlap → pipeline/ring_exchange → RTX 4090不支持multicast
- **RTX 4090**: DelayedScaling唯一可用; B≥4时1.48-1.59x加速; 无MXFP8/NVFP4
