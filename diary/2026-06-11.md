## 16:20 — FSDP vs DDP Scaling Benchmark OPT-125M (GPU实测)

### 结果

在8×RTX 4090上运行OPT-125M FSDP/DDP训练扩展基准测试:

| Config | Step Time | Per-GPU tok/s | Total tok/s | Speedup vs 1GPU |
|---------|-----------|---------------|-------------|------------------|
| 1 GPU   | 0.024s    | 42,112        | 42,112      | **1.00x**        |
| FSDP 2  | 0.063s    | 16,329        | 32,659      | 0.77x            |
| DDP 2   | 0.052s    | 19,693        | 39,386      | 0.94x            |
| FSDP 4  | 0.106s    | 9,656         | 38,626      | 0.92x            |
| DDP 4   | 0.095s    | 10,772        | 43,089      | 1.02x            |
| FSDP 8  | 0.109s    | 9,425         | 75,401      | 1.79x            |
| DDP 8   | 0.103s    | 9,985         | 79,882      | **1.90x**        |

### 关键发现

- **DDP > FSDP for 125M** (DDP comm ops更少→1 AllReduce vs FSDP 3 AllGather/RS)
- **Per-GPU吞吐量暴跌76%!** 42K→10K tok/s → comm overhead相对显著
- **总吞吐量近线性扩展** → 8GPU DDP=1.90x → 可接受
- **FSDP内存省24%** (1669→1274 MB) → 小模型不需要
- **125M vs 7B对比**: 125M 8GPU=1.79x(可接受) vs 7B=0.46x(灾难!)
- **原因**: comm_ratio 125M=12.5% vs 7B=75%

## 16:30 — LoRA Fine-tuning Benchmark OPT-125M (GPU实测)

### 关键发现: LoRA训练比Full FT慢37%!

| Method | Step Time | Throughput | Memory | Trainable |
|---------|-----------|------------|--------|-----------|
| Full FT  | 0.060s    | 97K tok/s  | 6,076  | 100%      |
| LoRA r=8 | 0.082s    | 72K tok/s  | 7,761  | 1.05%     |

**意外发现**: LoRA不是训练加速! PEFT adapter dispatch overhead → 37%慢!
但小batch(B=8)→内存省19%, 大batch(B=256)→反而多用28%内存!

### LoRA Merge = 关键

- 未合并推理: 2.51x慢 (14ms vs 5.7ms)
- 合并后推理: **与base相同** (5.62ms vs 5.71ms)
- **生产必须merge后推理!**

### LoRA Rank Sweep

步时间几乎恒定(~0.082s), 不受rank影响! adapter计算量negligible vs base model.
r=2→r=32: step 0.083→0.084s, memory 7740→7845MB (仅+105MB)

### RTX 4090 LoRA推荐

- 7B: LoRA必需(FT OOM), r=8 alpha=16, train→merge→INT4→FlashInfer
- 125M: FT更快, LoRA仅用于内存节省
- 生产: LoRA train → merge → quantize → serve

## 16:50 — Mixed Precision Training Benchmark (GPU实测)

### 关键发现: BF16是RTX 4090训练唯一正确选择!

76K模型:
- FP32=133 steps/s, BF16=178(**1.34x**), FP16 AMP=146(1.10x), BF16 AMP=209(**1.58x**)
- 所有精度稳定, BF16 AMP最快但76K太小→不代表性

2.28M模型(更重要):
- **BF16: 1.23x加速+39% eval(最高!)+37%内存省**
- FP16 AMP: 1.01x(**零加速!**) + eval 19%(**下降11%!**)
- BF16 AMP: 1.28x但eval 10%(**下降20%!**)

**结论**: BF16=RTX 4090训练最优! FP16 AMP是V100时代遗留→RTX 4090无收益+损精度
