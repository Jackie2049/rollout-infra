## 16:30 — LoRA Fine-tuning Benchmark OPT-125M (GPU实测)

### 关键发现: LoRA训练比Full FT慢37%!

| Method | Step Time | Throughput | Memory | Trainable |
|---------|-----------|------------|--------|-----------|
| Full FT  | 0.060s    | 97K tok/s  | 6,076  | 100%      |
| LoRA r=8 | 0.082s    | 72K tok/s  | 7,761  | 1.05%     |

**意外发现**: LoRA不是训练加速! PEFT adapter dispatch overhead → 37%慢!
但小batch(B=8)→内存省19%, 大batch(B=256)→反而多用28%内存!

### LoRA Merge = 关键

- 未合并推理: 2.51x慢 (14ms vs 5.7ms)
- 合并后推理: **与base相同** (5.62ms vs 5.71ms)
- **生产必须merge后推理!**

### LoRA Rank Sweep

步时间几乎恒定(~0.082s), 不受rank影响! adapter计算量negligible vs base model.
r=2→r=32: step 0.083→0.084s, memory 7740→7845MB (仅+105MB)

### RTX 4090 LoRA推荐

- 7B: LoRA必需(FT OOM), r=8 alpha=16, train→merge→INT4→FlashInfer
- 125M: FT更快, LoRA仅用于内存节省
- 生产: LoRA train → merge → quantize → serve

## 17:10 — Gradient Accumulation Benchmark (GPU实测)

### 关键发现: Gradient Accumulation不是免费的! 15x吞吐损失!

| Effective B | Real B Throughput | Accum Throughput | Ratio | Real Mem | Accum Mem |
|-------------|-------------------|------------------|-------|----------|-----------|
| 64          | 95,244 tok/s      | 6,263 tok/s      | **0.07x** | 1,471 MB | 352 MB |
| 32          | 47,591 tok/s      | 6,262 tok/s      | 0.13x | 872 MB | 353 MB |

**Accum吞吐=real/accum_steps**(线性衰减!), 但**内存恒定**(353MB regardless of accum!)

### LoRA+Accum=最大内存效率
- Eff B=32: Full FT=1,258MB→LoRA real=871MB→LoRA+accum=352MB → **72%内存省**
- 这是7B RTX 4090训练方案: B=1+accum=8→effective B=8→fits in 6GB

### 实用推荐
- 125M: 真实B=32/64(吞吐最高)
- 7B LoRA: B=4+accum=4(eff B=16)→内存352MB→可行
- 生产: LoRA r=8 + BF16 + B=4 accum=4 → merge → quantize → serve

## 17:50 — Activation Checkpointing Benchmark (GPU实测)

### 关键发现: Checkpointing=31%吞吐损失, 内存省11-45%(随batch增长)

| Config | Step Time | Memory | Mem Saving | Thput Cost |
|--------|-----------|--------|------------|------------|
| B=4 no_ckpt | 0.042s | 355 MB | — | — |
| B=4 ckpt    | 0.061s | 317 MB | **11%** | **31%** |
| B=32 no_ckpt| 0.042s | 986 MB | — | — |
| B=32 ckpt   | 0.062s | 636 MB | **35%** | **31%** |
| B=128 ckpt  | 0.063s | 1,799 MB | **45%** | **29%** |

**31%吞吐损失恒定**(recompute forward cost), 不受batch影响!
**内存省随batch增长**: B=4→11%, B=128→45% → saves activation memory

**Full FT+ckpt**: 仅省1%(optimizer states dominate→无用!)
**LoRA+ckpt**: 省35%→activations fraction大→有效!

**RTX 4090推荐**: 小模型不用ckpt; 7B LoRA B≥16用ckpt
